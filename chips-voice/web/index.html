Chips Voice

Two small web apps for the station. They run in your browser and talk to your own servers; nothing is stored here.

Voice Lab

Try voices, models, tone, effects and sound effects side by side. Audition host personas like CHIP, then apply them. Connects to a Chips Voice server.

Control Panel

Every station on one screen: now playing, skip, volume, playlists, broadcasts at the same moment, and copying host settings. Connects to the hub.
